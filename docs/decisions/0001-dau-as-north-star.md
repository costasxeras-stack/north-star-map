# 0001: DAUs as the North Star, with a monetisation counter-metric

**Status:** accepted, 0.1.0

## Context

I could pick DAUs, paid subscribers or bookings as the North Star. Duolingo itself steers by DAUs and has said so publicly.

## Decision

Keep DAUs as the North Star and put monetisation beside the tree as a counter-metric branch, with paid subscribers per DAU as the main check.

## Tradeoff

- A pure user metric can hide a business that monetises less per user, which is exactly what the 2026 numbers show
- A revenue North Star would push teams toward paywalls that hurt the habit
